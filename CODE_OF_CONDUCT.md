# Code of conduct

Soul Society is a technical commons. Participation requires curiosity,
precision, and respect.

Be direct about code and claims without attacking people. Welcome newcomers,
credit prior work, disclose conflicts, protect private reports, and make room
for disagreement grounded in evidence. Harassment, threats, discrimination,
doxxing, sexualized attention, and sustained bad-faith disruption are not
accepted.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
needed to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately to the
maintainer through the repository's security-advisory channel. Reports will be
handled as discreetly as practical.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when representing the project
elsewhere.
